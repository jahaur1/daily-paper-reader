## 摘要
本文提出了一种生成式人工智能（Gen AI）方法，用于在日前阶段预测爱尔兰电力系统上的最大单点注入（LSI）和最大单点输出（LSO），以辅助备用容量确定。该系统由爱尔兰输电系统运营商（TSO）EirGrid与GridZero.ai合作开发，利用GridZero.ai平台，在日前和日内能源市场关口关闭时间之前使用有限的数据，提供实时时间前最多38小时的准确预测。初步性能显示，其平均绝对百分比误差（MAPE）仅比使用完整市场数据（提前8小时）可能达到的结果高出1.1%。因此，如果将此方法集成到运行系统中并保持如此高的精度，备用采购成本可能会显著降低。结果还展示了人工智能驱动的资源规划对输电系统运营商的实用性和可扩展性。

## Abstract
This paper presents a generative artificial intelligence (Gen AI) approach for forecasting, at a day-ahead stage, the largest single infeed (LSI) and largest single outfeed (LSO) on the Irish power system to assist in reserve dimensioning. Developed collaboratively between EirGrid, the electric transmission system operator (TSO) for Ireland, and GridZero.ai using the GridZero.ai platform, the system delivers accurate forecasts up to 38 hours ahead of real-time using limited data available before the day-ahead and intra-day energy market gate closure timings. Initial performance demonstrates an accuracy with a mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) that is only 1.1\% higher than the results possible using full market data (8-hours ahead). Thus, if this approach is integrated into operational systems and such high levels of accuracy are maintained, reserve procurement costs could be significantly reduced. The results also demonstrate the practicality and extensibility of AI-powered resource planning for TSOs.
